# K8.4: bids and sells rest on the same token at the same time

Built 2026-10-22 on top of K8.3 (`../k8.3/`), at the owner's request ("every pool will have active buy and sell orders, so they need to be permitted to do that"). Not run on a testnet. Reviewed by independent adversarial reviews (every finding re-checked by a skeptic, no run of the code by the reviewers unless noted): the K8.4 review confirmed two findings (the global bid cap did not bind the first bid of a new ledger: fixed; a very low sell closes a token's bid side: disclosed below, not fixed). The review of the first park-and-resolve fallback confirmed six defects (the parked slice was not tied to its shares so a donation or one cheap fill could take it, cancels consumed the evidence first, a late joiner shared in it, an IPO holding without a sell could not resolve it): all fixed in this build, each with a test. A third review of the fixed fallback and of the raised limits was cut short twice by the machine running out of memory: its accounting lens found one more defect (one share count for several parked dividends misprices the first: fixed by tracking the worth of a share per parked dividend, with a test), its limits lens found nothing (it ran the paged reads, then four pages, against the real QX at 1, 256, 257, 512, 513, 768, 769, 1023 and 1024 orders) and its liveness lens found nothing; the path check (every registration goes through CheckSource, every credit resolves first, every sale is blocked by HoldingEscrow) I did myself by reading the call sites. NOT completed: the independent re-check of the per-share arithmetic and the lens that critiques the tests. The last change, doubling the limits again (32 per side per token, 12 per pool, 1,024 in all, five pages of QX's book), came after all the reviews: it is covered by the compile-time asserts and by the tests (a full ledger of 32 pools, a bid on the fifth page behind 1,100 orders), not by a separate review.

## What changed against K8.3
K8.3 let a token's ledger hold resting bids OR resting sells. K8.4 lets it hold BOTH.
- A ledger keeps two lists: **bids** (highest price first, as in K8.2) and **sells** (lowest price first, as in K8.3), up to 32 of each (K8.3 had 8; raised twice on the owner's request, see Limits). A holding keeps two order records: its bid (`qxBidPrice`, `qxBidTick`, `qxSeq`, `qxEscrow`) and its sell (`askPrice`, `askTick`, `askSeq`, `askEscrow`). One holding can therefore rest the part of its purchase that has not filled AND a sell of the shares that have.
- **What filled and what sold is read from QX's two order books**, never from the share count. Bid fills = what is booked as pending less what QX still lists for QPOOL, capped by what the universe holds beyond the recognised shares and the unsold sells. Sold shares = what the sells were booked for less what QX still lists.
- **The dividend hook now calls QX.** A payment on a ledger with orders must be split between the recognised shares, the bids' fills and the sells' unsold shares, and only QX's books tell the last two apart. The hook reads `EntityBidOrders` and `EntityAskOrders` of QPOOL (a callback may call a lower contract; QX reads only its own state, so the universe lock the hook runs under is not in the way). Slices: bids' fills to the bids that won them (highest price first), the sells' unsold shares to the sells that still hold them (from the back of the list), the rest (recognised shares, shares that belong to no order such as a donation, rounding) through the accumulator, to QPOOL revenue when there are no recognised shares.
- **If QX cannot be read inside the hook** the slice of the payment that belongs to the unrecognised shares is **parked**: `GetSource.unresolved` (QU, inside the ledger's escrow) with `unresolvedRateX64` (what one unrecognised share of the parked dividends is worth, summed over the dividends parked). The next `ReconcileHolding` of any holding of that ledger (anyone may call it, also on an IPO holding that rests no sell), `CancelQxBid` or `CancelQxAsk` splits it first, with QX's books as they are then and per share at that rate: the unsold sells first, then the fills of the bids, never more than the slice; what belongs to no order (a donation) goes to the recognised holders, as the hook sends it. While a slice is parked no new holding may join the ledger (`SETTLEMENT_PENDING`; call `ReconcileHolding` first) and sales and seat trades of the ledger wait. The first version of this fallback had six defects found by an adversarial review (the slice was not bound to its shares, so a donation or one cheap fill could take it; cancels consumed the evidence before it was split; a late joiner shared in it; an IPO holding without a sell could not resolve it); all six are fixed and each has a test that fails without its fix.
- Everything else is as in K8.3: sells are voted like any sale and carried out by an operator, silent fills are credited by anyone's `ReconcileHolding`, `CancelQxAsk` / `CancelQxBid` take orders back, the fee bound, the QPAY ban, the EvictSquat and BuyPosition guards.

## Decisions I took that you may veto
1. **A new bid must be priced below QPOOL's own lowest resting sell of that token** (`SELF_CROSS`, 44). QX sees QPOOL as one entity: a bid at or above its own sell would be matched against it, and what that sell is paid would be taken for a refund of the buy. Consequence: while pool B rests a sell at 2,000, pool A cannot bid 2,000 or more on the same token (it can bid 1,999). A sell INTO QPOOL's own resting bid is allowed: it is an internal trade between pools, credited like any sale (the immediate sale has always handled it).
2. **One pool holds at most 12 of the 32 places of one side of one token** (`POOL_SLOTS`, 45), so that one pool cannot take a whole side. Change `QPOOL_MAX_QX_ORDERS_PER_POOL_PER_SIDE` if you want another share.
3. **The hand-over fee** of a resting sell that fills nothing is paid from QPOOL's revenue (K8.3), with the INSUFFICIENT_FEE refusal when revenue cannot pay it.
4. **A parked slice (QX unreadable at a dividend) blocks new holdings on that ledger** until anyone calls `ReconcileHolding` (a new holding would otherwise be paid part of a dividend that arrived before it existed). It is a rare path; the cost is one extra call.

## The biggest thing that is not proven
**The hook calling QX has never run on a node.** It runs in the local test harness (the real QX contract of the local core, 55 tests including exact dividend splits on a ledger with a recognised holding, a resting bid and a resting sell) and the core's own comments describe a callback calling a contract function as supported, but a real node can differ (lock order, cost). Suite V9 of the guide measures it. If QX cannot be read (it is in an error state, and then no QX trade works either) the hook does not guess and never substitutes QPOOL's booked numbers: it parks the part of the payment that belongs to the unrecognised shares (see the bullet "If QX cannot be read inside the hook" above; tested locally by putting QX into an error state). The reads cannot fail while QPOOL stays under its caps of 1,024 resting orders per side (QX lists an owner's orders 256 at a time and QPOOL reads up to five pages; a read whose every page is full is treated as unreadable).

## Limits and approximations (read before relying on it)
- **The credit of a sell uses an upper bound of QX's fee**, so a seller is credited up to (shares - fills) QU per price level less than QX paid (K8.3). The remainder goes to QPOOL revenue when the last resting sell is gone.
- **A hostile pool can close the BID side of a token.** The self-trade rule keys on the lowest resting sell of the token, whoever owns it: a pool that rests a sell of a dust share at a very low price (even 1 QU) makes every BUY_QX of that token by any pool fail with `SELF_CROSS` until that sell is sold or cancelled (anyone can cancel it after 72 hours; the pool can place it again at once). Sells, immediate sales, QSWAP buys and other tokens are not affected, and no QU or share is at risk. The real fix is to let a bid that crosses QPOOL's own sell trade with it and book that as an internal trade between the two pools (exact: one fill per price level, so the fee is known); not built, say if you want it (K8.5).
- **The hook's work grows**: two QX reads per payment per possession record on a ledger that has orders (one page of 256 orders each while QPOOL has at most 256 orders resting). Measure it (V9).
- A sell that reaches past the 256-bid window places only what the window takes. **Limits (raised in this build): at most 32 resting orders per side per token ledger (shared by all pools), at most 12 of them from one pool, and 1,024 resting bids and 1,024 resting sells in all.** QX lists an owner's orders 256 at a time; QPOOL now reads its books page by page (up to five pages, 1,280 orders; the caps leave room for the 32 orders an immediate trade adds for the length of one call), and a read whose every page is full is refused as unreadable rather than trusted. The cost: state grows (the 32-place lists), loops are longer, and a call that touches QX's books reads one page per 256 orders QPOOL has resting (one page until QPOOL has more than 256 orders). To change them: `QPOOL_MAX_QX_BIDS_PER_LEDGER` (a power of 2), `QPOOL_MAX_QX_ORDERS_PER_POOL_PER_SIDE`, `QPOOL_MAX_RESTING_ASKS` / `_BIDS` and `QPOOL_QX_READ_PAGES` (static_asserts keep them consistent).
- A new bid is refused (`SELF_CROSS`) while a sell that has already sold out is still listed (it leaves the list at the next reconcile, which anyone can call).
- `globalUnattributed` holds the proceeds of filled sells until a reconcile (invariant I7).
- A resting sell of the QPAY token is refused. EvictSquat refuses a holding that rests any order. A seat of a holding that rests a sell cannot be bought; the seat trade of another holding is not held back by dividends set aside for an order.
- Still counted from shares, not from QX: the quantities of one call (a new order's immediate fills, an immediate sale) are exact inside the transaction.

## State and ABI against K8.3
- `Holding` gains `askPrice`, `askTick`, `askSeq`, `askEscrow` (+32 bytes). `SourceLedger` loses `qxSide` and gains `askCount`, `askHold[32]`, `unresolved` and `unresolvedRateX64` (a uint128). **Measured: StateData 208,920,624 bytes (K8.3: 196,993,072, +11,927,552).** A fresh deployment is needed, as before.
- `GetSource` returns `askCount` where K8.3 returned `qxSide` (appended position unchanged) and appends `unresolved`; `GetHolding` appends `askPrice`, `askTick` (its `qxBidPrice` / `qxBidTick` are the bid's only). New codes `SELF_CROSS` (44) and `POOL_SLOTS` (45); `QX_SIDE_BUSY` (42) is unused. `CheckSource` takes the pool and the price of a bid.

## What was checked
Ran (gtests against the real QX code of the local core, in the scratch core): 55 tests pass (54 contract tests and 1 that prints the state size; 1 more is switched off, the obsolete squat test): the tests of K8.2 and K8.3 adapted, and new ones for a bid and a sell resting together and filling (exact amounts), the dividend split between a recognised holding, a bid and a sell, the self-trade rules, one holding resting a bid and a sell, a cancelled bid that must not retire a holding that rests a sell, the per-pool places (12 of 32) and the global bid cap on a new ledger, thirty-two pools resting sells on one token, a bid and a sell that sit beyond the first 256 orders of QPOOL on QX (the second page is read), a bid on the fifth and last page (1,100 orders of QPOOL ahead of it), and the parked-dividend fallback (a slice parked while QX cannot be read is paid per share with QX's books: after a failed read, with fills before and after the dividend, with a donated share, after two parked dividends, resolved first by CancelQxBid and by CancelQxAsk, no new holding while a slice is parked). Mutation control: with the fixes switched off, 5 of those tests and 2 IPO probes fail. IPO probes on the core's own finishIPOs and assetsEndEpoch: a settled IPO holding rests a sell (T5), the cancel of its only sell resolves a parked slice (T6) and an IPO holding without a sell resolves one (T7) pass; the K8 settlement tests (T1, T4) and name tests (A to E) pass; F, G and H fail exactly as on K8, K8.2 and K8.3 (they expect return code 40 where the code is 41, and H expects a manual settle after the epoch-start settlement has done it). Checks: check_contract.sh (compile, link, smoke), lint_contract.py, release_gate.py (the season build fails the gate on purpose).
Not run: anything on a real node (above all the hook's QX calls); MSVC; the official contract verifier; the hand-over-fee refusal; several buyers hitting one price level; the 1,024-order caps on a real node (the local test puts 300 and 1,100 orders of QPOOL on QX, so the second and the fifth page are read); QSWAP interplay; fills of 3e12 QU or more; a sale beyond the 256-bid window. The season plans are generator-checked only.
