# K8.3: a pool can rest a SELL limit order on QX

Built 2026-10-22 on top of K8.2 (`../k8.2/`). Not run on a testnet; no independent review (no reviewer agents, at the owner's request). Your testnet is the test.

## What it does
A new proposal kind, **SELL_QX_LIMIT (7)**: price = the limit, shares = the size (at most the holding's free `shares`), `quAmount` must be 0. It is voted and tallied like any sale and an operator (the creator or an admin) executes it.
- What the visible bids pay at or above the limit **fills at once** and is credited from the balance, exactly as a SELL_QX.
- The rest **rests on QX's book** at the limit price. Its shares move from the holding's `shares` to the new `askShares` (they are out of the voting/earning weight but still sit with QPOOL until a buyer takes them).
- A buyer who later hits the order pays QPOOL **silently** (no call into QPOOL): the shares leave, the QU arrives and the hook files it under `globalUnattributed`. Anyone's `ReconcileHolding` credits it: the sold shares and their proceeds go to the holdings in the ledger's list, **lowest price first and, at one price, the order placed first** (QX sells in that order; at one price QX merges QPOOL's orders into one and QPOOL serves them earliest first, as for bids). The credit is shares sold x price, less QX's fee, less QPOOL's 1% management fee, taken out of `globalUnattributed` and never more than it holds.
- Dividends on the **unsold** shares are set aside for the sells that still hold them (from the back of the list, since the cheapest were sold first) and paid when that sell is credited or cancelled.
- **CancelQxAsk{poolId, holdingId}** (procedure 25) takes the unsold part off QX's book and puts the shares back into the holding. Operators any time; anybody once the order is older than 72 hours (the same limit as bids). What sold meanwhile is credited first.
- Works for IPO-won contract shares as well as bought, deposited and token holdings.

## Two things I decided that you may veto
1. **A token's ledger holds resting BIDS or resting SELLS, never both.** While any pool has a sell resting on token X, a BUY_QX of X is refused at Propose (`QX_SIDE_BUSY`, 42); while a bid rests, a SELL_QX_LIMIT is refused at Execute. Immediate SELL_QX and SELL_QSWAP are not affected. Reason: shares that arrive (a bid filling) and shares that leave (a sell filling) both show up only as "QPOOL holds more/less than its holdings add up to"; with both open at once QPOOL could not tell which pool's order did what, and would credit the wrong pool. Lifting it would need QPOOL to read QX's order book on every fill; not built. The block ends when the orders fill or are cancelled (anyone after 72 hours).
2. **The hand-over fee is QPOOL's.** A sell needs the shares under QX's management. If a holding's shares are QPOOL-managed (deposits) or QSWAP-managed, handing them over costs QX's 100 QU per source. For a SELL_QX_LIMIT that fills nothing at once, that fee comes out of QPOOL's own revenue (`pendingRevenue`), not out of any member (a sale that fills at once still nets it from its proceeds as before). Repeating it for the same shares costs nothing (they stay QX-managed).

## Limits and approximations (read before relying on it)
- **The credit uses an upper bound of QX's fee, so a seller is credited a little less than QX paid.** QX charges floor(price x shares x 0.3%) + 1 per FILL and the number of fills is not visible (a fill that pays QPOOL nothing never reaches the hook). QPOOL credits gross - (floor(gross x 0.3%) + shares sold at that price level): every fill moves at least one share, so this is never below QX's real fees and a ledger can never be credited more than its own fills paid in. The price: up to (shares - fills) QU per level less than QX really paid (about 0.1% of the proceeds when a few hundred QU-priced shares sell in one fill; more at tiny prices; nothing is credited for a level whose fee bound exceeds its gross). The remainder stays in `globalUnattributed` and goes to QPOOL revenue when the last resting sell of QPOOL is gone. (The first K8.3 build estimated one fill per level; an independent review showed that a buyer could then draw another ledger's proceeds out of the shared counter at about 1% cost, so it was replaced.)
- **QX's book, not the share count, says what sold.** A single donated share can no longer leave a sold sell booked for ever. If QX's book cannot be read, nothing is credited that time.
- **Bids now ask QX too.** `ReconcileHolding`, `CancelQxBid` and the BUY_QX Execute decide what filled from QX's bid book (what QPOOL has booked as resting minus what QX still lists), and credit no more than the universe really holds beyond the recognised shares. A donated share is no longer credited as a fill (it was in K8.2). A book read that cannot be trusted (it fails, or it fills QX's whole 256-order window) credits nothing and refuses a cancel; QPOOL therefore keeps at most 128 resting bids and 128 resting sells in all (`CAPACITY` beyond). Still counted from shares: the dividend set-aside in the hook (it cannot call QX), where a donated share can steer the dividend QPOOL receives on it to a resting order's holding instead of the recognised holdings (capped at what is pending), and the quantities of one call (the new order's immediate fills, an immediate sale), which are exact inside the transaction.
- At most **8 resting sells per ledger** and **128 in all** (QPOOL reads its asks from QX's window of 256 orders). A holding rests one sell at a time. If a sale reaches past the 256-bid window the part beyond it is not placed.
- `globalUnattributed` is no longer 0 between calls: it holds the proceeds of filled sells until a reconcile (guide invariant I7 changed).
- **A hostile pool can keep a token in sell mode.** Because a token's ledger holds bids or sells, never both, a pool that keeps a sell resting on token X (cancelled by anyone after 72 hours, placed again by its own vote) makes every other pool's BUY_QX of X fail with `QX_SIDE_BUSY`; the mirror image holds for a resting bid and SELL_QX_LIMIT. The other pool's immediate sales and QSWAP buys are not affected. Removing this needs per-fill attribution from QX's own book; not built.
- A resting sell of the QPAY token is refused (BuyAndBurnQpay would bid against it). EvictSquat refuses a holding that rests a sell. **A seat of a holding that rests a sell cannot be bought** (BuyPosition returns SETTLEMENT_PENDING; it can be offered, and the purchase works once the sell is sold out or taken back): a buyer cannot bind `askShares` the way he binds `shares`, and anyone's ReconcileHolding would turn a filled sell into the seller's proceeds before the purchase lands. The dividends set aside for one holding's unsold shares do not hold back the seat trades of other holdings of the ledger.
- A resting sell that needs the hand-over to QX's management and fills nothing at once is refused with INSUFFICIENT_FEE when QPOOL's `pendingRevenue` is below the hand-over fee.
- Seat trading: `GetPosition` shows `holdingAskShares`; `offeredShares` is only the free shares.
- A passed SELL_QX_LIMIT whose operators never carry it out lapses like any mandate.

## State and ABI against K8.2
- `Holding` gains `askShares` (+8 bytes), `SourceLedger` gains `qxSide` and `askTotal` (+16), `StateData` gains `restingAsks` and `restingBids`. **Measured: StateData 195,682,336 -> 196,993,072 bytes (+1,310,736).** A fresh deployment is needed, as before.
- New: proposal kind 7, procedure 25 `CancelQxAsk`, return codes `QX_SIDE_BUSY` (42) and `NO_PENDING_ASK` (43). `QX_BID_PENDING` (25) also means "this holding already rests a sell". Appended outputs: `GetHolding.askShares`, `GetPosition.holdingAskShares`, `GetSource.qxCount/qxSide/askTotal`, `GetStats.restingAsks/restingBids`, `ReconcileHolding.askShares`.
- Unchanged semantics: bids (K8.2), operators (K8.1), names and settlement (K8).

## What was checked
Reviewed: an adversarial review of the first build (five lenses, 25 agents, each finding re-checked by a skeptic) confirmed 19 reports that come down to eight defects; a second review of the fixes (three lenses, 8 agents) confirmed two more (a seat bought against a sell that fills in the same window, and earmarked dividends blocking unrelated seat trades). All ten are fixed in this build and have tests (cross-ledger drain, phantom ask, EvictSquat, QPAY, seat sniping, seat trades), except the hand-over guard and the hostile-pool block (disclosed above).
Ran: `QPool-K8.3.h` passes `check_contract.sh` (compile, link, smoke run of INITIALIZE and the 10 read-only functions), `lint_contract.py` and `release_gate.py` (the season build fails the gate as intended). The devkit files are generated by `make_devkit.py` (which re-reads its output); the local harness only compiles the production-named file.
Ran (gtests against the real QX code of the local core): 39 tests pass (38 contract tests and 1 that prints the state size; 1 more is switched off, the obsolete squat test): the 19 tests of K8.2, 1 donated-share bid test and 18 resting-sell tests, plus the size print.
New tests: a resting sell fills in part and is credited exactly (59,819 arrives, 59,221 credited), a second reconcile writes nothing, a sold-out holding retires and its ledger goes; what the bids pay fills at once and the rest rests; two pools sell cheapest first (exact proceeds, counter used up); two pools at one price serve the earlier first; cancel by creator, admin and (after the age limit) a stranger; exclusivity both ways with the state byte for byte unchanged; one sell per holding; an immediate sale of another holding while an ask rests; eight pools fill the ledger, the ninth is refused and one reconcile credits all eight; dividends on contract shares are set aside for the asks that still hold them (reverse order) and paid.
Ran (IPO probe on the core's own finishIPOs and assetsEndEpoch, not a node): a settled IPO holding rests a sell, the dividend on its unsold shares is set aside and paid, a buyer's fill is credited, the sold-out holding retires. The K8 IPO-settlement tests (T1, T4) and name tests (A to E) still pass; F, G and H fail exactly as on K8 and K8.2 (they expect return code 40 where the code is 41, and H expects a manual settle after the epoch-start settlement has done it).
The season plans are generator-checked only (coverage asserted, no problems).
Not run: anything on a real node; MSVC; the official contract verifier; the hand-over fee case (U15); the several-buyers-at-one-level case (U7); the hand-over guard (INSUFFICIENT_FEE); the 128-in-all cap; a fill of 3e12 QU or more (QX's other fee formula is implemented, not run); the interplay with QSWAP management hand-overs; a sale beyond the 256-bid window.
